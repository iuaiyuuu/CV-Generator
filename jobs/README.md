# jobs/

Save job descriptions you want to apply for. **Private — git-ignored.**

## Two ways to add a job

1. **Paste it into the chat with your AI.** The AI saves it here as `<company>_<role>.md` using `job.template.md`, then continues with the CV.
2. **Paste it into a file yourself**, e.g. `acme_data-analyst.md`, copied from `job.template.md`.

Paste the **full** text: responsibilities, requirements, nice-to-haves, and company info. A link or a title alone is not enough, because the AI can only tailor to what it can read. If the page needs a login, copy the text from it.

For a program, fellowship, or scholarship, also paste the deadline and the eligibility lines ("who we are looking for"). The AI checks the deadline and whether your level fits before writing anything (`workflows/generate-cv.md`, step 2b).
